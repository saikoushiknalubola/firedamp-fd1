# Patent notes (draft, not filed)

Team Chakra, Firedamp Dynamics. Working notes for a possible provisional application. Nothing here is a claim of novelty until the prior-art search below is done.

## Draft claim ideas

1. A wellhead unit for a methane drainage borehole that measures methane concentration, oxygen concentration and flow at that borehole, and reduces the suction on that borehole when the measured methane falls below a routing threshold, so as to hold that borehole's gas above the threshold.
2. The unit of idea 1, where methane is measured two ways: directly by an infrared sensor, and indirectly from oxygen as CH4 ≈ 1 − O2/20.95 for a binary seam-gas/air mixture. The unit closes the borehole valve when the two disagree by more than a set margin.
3. The unit of idea 1, where the sample stream through the sensing chamber is driven by the pressure drop across the unit's own metering orifice, with no sample pump.
4. The unit of idea 1, where the flow reading is corrected using a mixture density calculated from the measured composition, and the corrected methane volume is logged per borehole.
